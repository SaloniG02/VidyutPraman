# VidyutPraman

## Digital Battery Passport & Circular Economy Protocol for Indian EV Fleets

VidyutPraman is a prototype digital battery passport and verification platform designed to improve transparency and traceability in the lifecycle of electric vehicle (EV) batteries.

## Problem

Used EV batteries can be difficult to evaluate independently. Buyers and other stakeholders may not have access to reliable battery history, diagnostic information, or a standardized record of battery condition.

This creates an information gap when batteries are bought, sold, reused, repurposed, or recycled.

## Solution

VidyutPraman provides a structured digital record for an EV battery, bringing together battery identity, health information, diagnostic results, lifecycle history, and verification data.

The platform is designed around:

- Digital Battery Passport
- Battery identity and lifecycle records
- Battery health information
- State of Health (SOH)
- Remaining Useful Life (RUL)
- Diagnostic information
- Battery condition classification
- Verification and traceability
- Second-life and recycling workflows

## MVP

The current project is an MVP demonstrating the concept of a digital battery passport for EV batteries.

The system is intended to provide a simple interface through which battery information can be recorded, viewed, and verified.

## Key Objective

The core objective of VidyutPraman is to reduce the information gap between battery owners, buyers, dealers, recyclers, and other stakeholders by creating a structured and traceable battery record.

## Technology

The project is currently implemented as a web-based MVP and is designed with future integration possibilities for:

- Battery Management System (BMS) data
- Battery diagnostic hardware
- Battery health estimation
- Digital verification
- Lifecycle tracking
- Second-life battery applications
- Recycling workflows

## Project Status

**Prototype / MVP**

This project is currently intended for demonstration and further development.

## Disclaimer

VidyutPraman is a prototype concept. Battery health values, classifications, and diagnostic outputs should not be treated as certified measurements unless they are produced and validated using appropriate diagnostic equipment and procedures.
